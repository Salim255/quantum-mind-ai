PRACTICE

Test what you understand.

Use quizzes to turn what you have learned into active understanding. Practice concepts, test your reasoning, and discover where you can go deeper.

WHY PRACTICE

Learning becomes stronger when you use it.

Quizzes give you a chance to retrieve ideas, reason through problems, and check whether a concept is truly clear. They are designed to complement the lessons, not replace them.

Recall what you learned

Bring concepts back from memory and strengthen your ability to recognize and use them.

Reason through problems

Go beyond remembering definitions by applying ideas and working through different situations.

Find what needs attention

Use your results to recognize gaps in understanding and return to the lessons when something needs more work.

WAYS TO PRACTICE

Choose the kind of practice you need.

Start with a broad review or focus your attention on a specific topic. Both paths are designed to reinforce what you have learned.

General quiz Test your understanding across multiple topics with a balanced mix of questions. Explore by topic Choose a specific topic and practice the concepts you want to understand more deeply.

Practice, review, understand, repeat.

A quiz can show you what is clear and what is not. When a question exposes a gap, return to the relevant lesson, strengthen the idea, and try again.
